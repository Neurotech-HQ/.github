# Security policy

We take the security of our products and our customers' data seriously. Thank you for helping us keep them safe.

## In scope

Sarufi, Ghala and Ghala SMS, Snippe, Sema (SemaCall), Neurotech Booking, Neurotech Accounts, and their APIs, apps and public repositories.

## Report a vulnerability

Please report privately. Do not open a public issue.

- Use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's Security tab (a private advisory), or
- Email **security@neurotech.africa**.

Tell us what is affected, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do. A short proof of concept helps.

## What to expect

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| We confirm we have your report | within 2 working days |
| We tell you how serious we judge it | within 5 working days |
| We fix critical and high issues | within 30 days, usually sooner |
| We tell you it is fixed, and credit you if you wish | when the fix ships |

## Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against you, or ask others to, for research that:

- uses only your own accounts or test accounts,
- does not read, change or keep other people's data beyond what proves the issue,
- does not disrupt service, send spam or move money, and
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before you tell anyone else.

If you reach customer data by accident, stop, do not keep it, and tell us.

## Out of scope

Denial of service, social engineering of staff, physical attacks, and reports from automated scanners with no proof of impact.
