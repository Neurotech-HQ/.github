# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who works on Neurotech products: staff, contractors, interns and outside contributors. It covers our repositories, issues, pull requests, chat, meetings and events, and any place you speak for Neurotech.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, even when you disagree. Argue about the work, never the person.
- Give and take feedback in good faith. Review code, not character.
- Assume people are acting in good faith, and ask before you judge.
- Protect the people we serve. Customer data is theirs, not ours. Follow the [AI and data handling guideline](policies/ai-and-data-handling.md).
- Own your mistakes. Say what went wrong early, so we can fix it together.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults or threats, in public or in private.
- Discrimination on grounds of tribe, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other personal trait.
- Sexual language or attention that is not welcome.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Looking at, copying or sharing customer data without a work reason.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@neurotech.africa**, or speak to any member of the leadership team in private. If your concern is about someone on that team, write to another member.

We reply within 3 working days. We keep the report private, and share it only with the people who must act on it.

## What happens next

The leadership team reviews the report and talks to the people involved, then decides the outcome. Depending on how serious it is, that may be:

1. A private word, with a clear note of what must change.
2. A formal warning, kept on record.
3. Losing access to repositories, chat or events for a set time.
4. A permanent ban, or for staff, action under the employment contract.
